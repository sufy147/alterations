How It Works

1. Submit Your Request

Complete the online form with details about your garment and the alteration required.

2. We Review Your Request

We'll assess the information provided and contact you if any additional details are needed.

3. Receive Your Quote

You'll receive an estimated price and turnaround time for the work.

4. Arrange Drop-Off or Collection

Bring your garment to us or arrange a convenient collection method.

5. Alteration Completed

Your garment is professionally altered with attention to detail and craftsmanship.

6. Collection & Final Check

Collect your garment and ensure you're completely happy with the fit.
